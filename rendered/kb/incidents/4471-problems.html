  • parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. Our tenants write the day first, so day and month swap: 7 May lands on 5 July.
  • hint: the booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type what the hint says, and the form reads it day first. Fixing the parser does not touch this; the hint must change.
  • shift: the date moves by one day, not a swap. The same meeting shows Tuesday in Lisbon and Monday in Toronto. A time zone bug. The desk already listed these reports as one-day moves in desk-notes.json; the old table still counts them under parser.

How the reports were counted

  • The ticket export repeats some rows across its pages; each report id counts once.
  • A report is placed by its tenant and the way it came in, not by its intake tag. Intake tags hint on every report from a US-English browser, which says nothing about the cause.

Lessons

  • Same subject is not same problem. Read a sample of bodies before calling a big ticket known.
  • Count by cause, and file one ticket per cause.
